What is Gravity SandBox?

A playground where gravity takes orders from you

Gravity SandBox is a first-person physics sandbox. Point at a crate and make it fall upward. Switch off gravity for the whole room and watch everything drift. Walk up a wall and across the ceiling, drop bubbles of zero-g, tie objects together with Newton’s law of attraction, or simply pick things up with your mind and throw them.

There are no levels, scores or timers. You get four handmade maps, seven powers that combine with each other and a map builder. The only goal is to find out what happens. Play alone, or invite up to four friends and break physics together.

  • ◎Real physicsEvery object has a real mass and its own gravity, recalculated 120 times a second. Heavy things are hard to throw, light things fly.
  • ⇅Powers that combineFlip the world sideways, then walk on the new floor. Put a bubble in a corridor, then throw a sofa through it.
  • ✦Build and shareMake your own maps, test them in a second and share them with a secret link or with friends.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is Gravity SandBox free?

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

Which computers can run it?

The desktop app runs on Windows 10 and 11 and on most 64-bit Linux systems. On a Mac, or anywhere else, you can play in a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox. You need a keyboard and mouse; phones and tablets are not supported.

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can play every map and power in singleplayer. A free account lets you save your own maps, add friends, and host or join rooms.

How does multiplayer work?

One player hosts a room on any map and shares the six-letter code or an invite link. Up to five players can join. The host’s computer runs the physics, so the player with the strongest PC and connection should host.

Can app and browser players play together?

Yes. Accounts, friends, rooms and saved maps are shared between the desktop app and the browser version.

A friend can’t join my room. What can I do?

Players connect directly to each other. Some strict networks (schools, offices, some mobile hotspots) block this. Try another network, or let the friend on the open network host.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Great! Tell us on GitHub. A short description and, for bugs, what you did just before help a lot.
