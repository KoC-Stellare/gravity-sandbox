Free for Windows. Your account, saved maps and friends are the same in the app and in the browser.

Download installer (.exe) Portable version (.zip)

Not on Windows?

Play the same game in your browser on Windows, macOS or Linux, with nothing to install.

Play in browser

Needs a desktop computer with keyboard and mouse, and a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Get playing in three steps

  1. DownloadClick “Download installer” above. The file is about 100 MB.
  2. InstallOpen the file. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info and then Run anyway: the game is new and not code-signed yet.
  3. PlayStart the tutorial in The Lab, or jump straight into a map. Create a free account to save maps and play with friends.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can play singleplayer. A free account lets you save your maps, add friends and host or join rooms.

Can app and browser players play together?

Yes. Rooms, invites and friends work across both.

How do I update?

Download the latest installer from this page and run it. Your settings and account stay.
